# Attribution and reuse

Historical references and photograph-specific credits are shown in `dist/sources.html` and `dist/assets/photos.json`.

The India boundary derives from the DataMeet India community’s Survey of India dataset. The combined map boundary database uses ODbL with DataMeet and Natural Earth attribution; its source links and licence details appear on the sources page. These terms apply to the geographic database, separately from the historical event records.

Leaflet 1.9.4 is bundled under its BSD 2-Clause licence, and Leaflet.markercluster 1.5.3 under its MIT licence. Their original copyright and licence notices remain in the bundled asset headers. Street-map tiles use CARTO and OpenStreetMap attribution in the map.

The audio guide is newly produced editorial narration using the synthesised Indian English Prabhat voice. It is not a historical recording of Swami Vivekananda.

Third-party material retains its original rights and stated reuse terms. No blanket licence is asserted for the complete collection.
